# ADR-0024: Deterministic Evidence and Claim derivation

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-09-05

## Context and task contract

M2-S06 is integrated on protected `main` at `b16ee30`, making M2-S07 the earliest eligible slice. Authenticated remote readback found no open pull request, and the synchronized clean tree has no competing branch or worktree claim. This task is claimed by `feat/m2-s07-evidence-claim-derivation`.

Traceability is `M2-S07`, `FMU-FR-004` through `FMU-FR-006`, `FMU-FR-020`, `FMU-NFR-005`, `FMU-NFR-006`, `FMU-NFR-011`, PROJECT_SPEC Sections 8 through 15, PROTOCOL Sections 2.1/2.2 and 5.2/5.3, ARCHITECTURE Sections 5.2/5.3 and 11/12, SECURITY_PRIVACY T-04/T-05/T-06/T-10/T-12 and Sections 8/10/11, ADR-0008, ADR-0021, and ADR-0022. No behavioral `FMU-E-*` applies because this slice produces private Evidence and Claim state but does not change a consumer response, owner correction, or sharing behavior.

The observable result is a pure implementation-internal Community derivation boundary that converts only structured source-language observations from an authentic M2-S05 snapshot into exact public Evidence records, aggregates them into conservative project-scoped Claims, exposes stable fingerprints and deterministic invalidation against a prior authentic derivation, and never emits high confidence or `demonstrated-depth`. In scope are a closed versioned derivation request, stable opaque identifiers, injected observation/derivation/staleness times, repository-to-project binding, bounded limitations and evidence references, output validation against the accepted Evidence/Claim/Profile payload contracts, adversarial unit and real-chain integration tests, this ADR, and synchronized documentation.

Hard non-goals are natural-language or dependency-name interpretation, arbitrary taxonomy configuration, global or adjacent inference, declarations, corrections/disputes, owner UI/CLI, persistence orchestration, Demand Profile production, Provider/MCP/adapter changes, network/model access, repository writes, public schema changes, dependencies, release, or migration. The implementation-internal derivation snapshot starts at `0.1.0`; no released or persisted data requires migration. Allowed effects are local code/document edits, temporary synthetic repositories, deterministic checks, a short-lived branch, and the already authorized pull request/squash merge and branch deletion. Stop on invented capability/depth, a missing provenance edge, elevation above upstream ceilings, content/path leakage outside private Evidence, unbounded output, a public-contract/dependency change, ownership conflict, unresolved high-severity finding, or protected CI failure.

## Decision

- Keep derivation inside `@fork-me-up/community-provider`. Extend each private M2-S05 source-risk record with its already collected normalized source language or `null`; this remains structural pre-Evidence metadata and does not itself assert capability. Mark source-risk snapshots as authentic in process so forged records cannot enter derivation.
- Accept one exact `0.1.0` request with injected `sourceObservedAt`, `derivedAt`, and `staleBefore` timestamps plus a complete unique repository-to-project mapping. The source observation cannot follow derivation, the stale threshold cannot follow derivation, and a refresh cannot move observation or derivation time backward. No wall clock, locale, random identifier, filesystem, process, or network is read.
- Produce Evidence only for normalized source-file languages, using `language.<language>` as the limited deterministic capability signal. Documents, manifest presence, dependency names, scripts, prose, paths, and annotations never create capabilities. Each Evidence ID is stable across content revisions from subject, repository, safe relative reference, and capability; its fingerprint covers the relevant content digest, source-risk/authorship state, fixed limitations, and extractor version but excludes collection time and unrelated repository-head movement.
- Preserve the source-risk strength ceiling and the upstream authorship depth/confidence ceilings, and map only a direct target association to Evidence `attributed`, or recognized collaboration to `coauthored`; bot-only input maps to `bot`, and every other combination maps to `unknown`. Only attributable/coauthored Evidence carries the opaque subject. Risk, authorship, provenance, automated-language, and truncation limitations are fixed, deterministically ordered, unique, and capped to the public maximum with an explicit omission marker.
- Aggregate by project and exact capability. At least one attributable/coauthored observation may produce `demonstrated`; otherwise the result is `insufficient-evidence`, never a negative-knowledge state. One or weak attributable signal yields at most `exposure`; two distinct moderate attributable source observations may yield `practical-use` only when both upstream authorship ceilings permit practical use. Confidence is at most medium and also respects the authorship ceiling; `demonstrated-depth` is never emitted. Evidence references are deterministically ranked and capped at the public maximum with a visible Claim limitation.
- Keep every generated Claim project-scoped. Stable Claim IDs derive from subject, project, and capability. Claim freshness uses the injected source observation and stale threshold. The Claim fingerprint covers its state, depth, confidence, selected Evidence fingerprints, staleness, and fixed limitations, but is internal metadata rather than a public Claim field.
- When an authentic prior derivation is supplied, compare stable Evidence and Claim identities. Missing Evidence reports `source-unavailable`; changed Evidence fingerprints report `fingerprint-changed`. Missing Claims report `no-longer-supported`; retained Claims report `evidence-invalidated`, `support-changed`, or `freshness-changed` as appropriate. New observations do not invalidate prior state, and no prior snapshot yields empty invalidation lists.
- Validate the completed project-reference, Evidence, and Claim graph through the exact accepted Profile payload boundary before returning a detached deeply immutable snapshot. Any forged prior/source snapshot, mapping mismatch, malformed timestamp, bound violation, schema/reference failure, or subject mismatch returns a fixed content-free error and no partial output.

## Consequences and alternatives

The first Community Evidence/Claim producer is intentionally useful but narrow: supported source extensions can establish only project-local language observations, and repeated direct moderate files whose authorship ceilings permit it can establish at most medium-confidence practical use. Risky, collaborative, shared, bot, or unknown work remains visibly capped without being promoted. Stable IDs plus semantic fingerprints distinguish an unchanged source from a changed or unavailable one without invalidating every file for an unrelated head revision.

Inferring frameworks from dependency presence, interpreting prose/imports, promoting a single commit to depth, combining projects into a global claim, converting unknown authorship to personal support, producing adjacency automatically, adding confidence scores, or using time/randomness implicitly is rejected. Correction precedence remains M2-S10, multi-repository refresh/cache-origin orchestration remains M2-S09, and persistence composition remains a caller responsibility until those slices.
